FROZEN EMPIRICAL RESULTS · SINGLE REVIEWER
Where the detector fails to generalize
The 36 development examples informed rule design. The 40 held-out intermediate turns are trajectory-disjoint, from the same corpus, and use a single reviewer with scope ambiguity. Agent-ClaimCheck was adapted for these domains; its shipped patterns skipped all cases. Its final-message focus and unrun LLM-judge mode limit the comparison.
Recorded receipt-binding repair — separate post-hoc study
42 selected receipt/action pairs from 28 trajectories; 126 corrupted variants are dependent. All 161 original receipts with valid request/response IDs agreed. Preserving 893 verdicts measures output stability, not accuracy. This repair does not improve claim detection.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARIES · SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY
10 · What this does and does not prove


Verified here
Deterministic reproduction of a claim-time ambiguity; executed read-only probes at different simulated times; successful data intake, first-pass human annotation and a published baseline execution.
Not demonstrated yet
Novelty versus AgentEval / Progress Drift / LIMBO; robust NLP extraction accuracy; independent two-reviewer agreement; a held-out strong-baseline improvement (the frozen results are negative); a general-purpose automatic evidence planner.


Remaining testable question: Can a claim-centered tool systematically choose a low-cost, admissible follow-up observation that resolves the specific claimed obligation, while refusing to certify what the observation cannot establish? Existing verification and wait policies are strong prior art; this remains a hypothesis.




Scientific scope: task-specific ACK checks; absence of a tool ACK is UNKNOWN, never proof of failure; simulator mail “sent” ≠ real-world delivery confirmation. An experimental rule-based field linker is available; no trained NLP extractor, held-out improvement or independent adjudicated gold is claimed. A single-reviewer real-trajectory study and sixteen scripted simulator probe executions are reported as separate, pre-existing experiments, with hash-pinned aggregate source files. The browser executes the receipt stress test locally; it replays the historical simulator experiments.
